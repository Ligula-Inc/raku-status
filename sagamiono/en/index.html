The water does not
come from here.

Dig under this building and you will not find a hot spring. So we carry it in, every day, from a valley an hour and a half away. Yugawara, the oldest hot spring in eastern Japan.

First written
AD 700s
Spring
Kamiya, Yugawara

You stay warm

Yugawara water has long been said to keep you warm after you leave the bath. Good for the night before you sleep.

“The healing water”

For centuries it was known for bruises and cuts; in the Edo period it was ranked third among the hot springs of eastern Japan.

Soft on the skin

Said to leave the skin soft. Clear and colourless.

We filmed the journey.

From the spring, over the mountains, to the station. 55 seconds, with music.
