What to bring, and how you pay

Can I come with nothing?
Yes. Shampoo, conditioner, body soap and hairdryers are provided. A towel set (bath and face) is ¥220, or bring your own.
Do I pay on the way in?
On the way out. Everything you use inside is recorded on your shoe-locker key, and the machine by the exit adds it up.
Do I need to book?
Not for the day bath. Yes for a capsule bed.

In the bath

I have tattoos.
You may enter if they are covered. Cover stickers are ¥220 each (10×15cm) at the front desk, up to three. If three do not cover them, we are sorry to say we cannot let you in. Please check before you travel.
Can I wear a swimsuit?
No. Everyone bathes undressed, and the baths are separated by gender.
Can I bring children?
From age 3, with a guardian of the same gender aged 18 or over. Mixed bathing is not possible from primary-school age.
Is the sauna extra?
It is included in the entry price. Sauna hats and ponchos are lent free, in limited numbers.
