Sauna

Dry sauna, cold bath and open-air seats, all on the roof. Included in the entry price.

See the pricesHow it works

How to do a round

Not a rule. Just what most people do, if you have not done this before.

  1. 1
    Sauna8–12 min

    Until your back is wet. Leave before it gets hard to breathe. The lower bench is cooler.

  2. 2
    Cold bath1–2 min

    Rinse the sweat off first. Once you are in, staying still is easier than moving.

  3. 3
    Open air5–10 min

    Dry off and sit on the roof. This part can be as long as you like.

That is one round. Two or three is usual.

What is happening

Heat opens the blood vessels near the skin. The cold bath closes them. Outside, they open again, slowly.

The light, floating feeling after that open–close–open is what people here call totonou.

Hotter is not better

This is not an endurance test. Leave while breathing is still easy. The same goes for the cold bath.

Please take care

Drink water before, during and after. Do not use the sauna after drinking alcohol.

Sit down straight away if you feel light-headed. If you have a heart or blood-pressure condition, ask your doctor first.

On a day when you feel unwell, skip it.

This describes what heat and cold do to the body. It is not a promise of any health benefit.
